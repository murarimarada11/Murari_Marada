1. What is CRM?
answer:1. What is CRM?
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. Think of it as a digital address book on steroids.
The Goal: It helps businesses keep track of everyone they talk to, what those people like, and what they have bought in the past.
The Problem it Solves: Instead of having information scattered in sticky notes, emails, and spreadsheets, everything is in one central place where the whole team can see it.
The Result: Better service for the customer and more organized work for the company.
2. Why Companies Use Salesforce
Salesforce is the world’s leading CRM platform because it lives in the cloud. Companies use it because:
No Hardware Needed: You don't need to buy expensive servers; you just log in through a web browser.
Everything in One Place: It connects sales, customer service, marketing, and even tech teams so everyone has the same "single source of truth" about a customer.
Scalability: Whether a company has 2 employees or 200,000, Salesforce can grow with them.
3. Key Salesforce Terms Explained
In Salesforce, data is organized into "Objects." Here are the three most important ones:
Account: This is a company or organization you do business with (e.g., "Apple" or "Target").
Contact: This is an individual person who works at an Account (e.g., "John Doe, Manager at Apple").
Opportunity: This is a potential deal or sale. It tracks how much money the deal is worth and how close it is to being finished.
